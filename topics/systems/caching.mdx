## Why cache?

A **cache** stores the result of expensive work (a database query, a computation,
a remote call) so that repeat requests can be served quickly. Caches are
frequently backed by a [hash table](/topics/data-structures/hash-tables) for
O(1) average lookup of cached entries by key.

<Callout type="note">
A cache is a bet that the near future looks like the recent past. When it does,
hit-rates are high and latency drops dramatically.
</Callout>

## Hits, misses, and eviction

A **hit** serves a stored result; a **miss** must do the work and (usually) store
it. Because caches are bounded, an <Term>eviction policy</Term> (e.g.{" "}
<Term>LRU</Term>) decides what to drop when full.
