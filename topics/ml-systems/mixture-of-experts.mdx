## The idea

A **dense** model runs every parameter for every token. A **Mixture of Experts**
model instead keeps many specialized sub-networks ("experts") and a small
**gating network** that routes each token to just the top-k of them. You get the
capability of a huge model at the cost of a small one — **sparse activation**.

The simulation below is a working MoE "factory": inject tokens, switch between
Top-1, Top-2, and Dense routing, and watch how many experts wake up, how compute
overhead and latency change, and how the gating weights are computed. Complete
the three quests to see the full trade-off.

<Callout type="note">
Notice the trade-off as you switch modes: Top-1 is cheapest (one expert), Top-2
balances cost and capability (the common default), and Dense runs everything —
maximum capability, maximum cost. Sparsity is what makes trillion-parameter
models practical to serve.
