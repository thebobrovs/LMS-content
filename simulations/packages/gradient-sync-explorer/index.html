Train on many accelerators and you pay a tax single-device training doesn't: every step, each device's gradient (a payload the size of the whole model) must be all-reduced — summed across every device — before the next step. That sync rides the device interconnect (the fabric wiring the accelerators together), and its time is bytes ÷ bandwidth. Scale up and watch it overtake the compute.
