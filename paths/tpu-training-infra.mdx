For cloud-infrastructure engineers, SREs, and platform admins who know
distributed systems, Linux, and GKE, but are new to AI accelerators. By the end
you can design, orchestrate, scale, optimize, and operate Cloud TPU training
clusters — and predict the effect of a slice shape, a sharding plan, or a failed
chip on a real training run.

**Level 100 — Foundations is live now.** Build the mental model: why TPUs exist and
how you reach one (the TPU VM), how the hardware *evolved by chasing bottlenecks*,
how a TPU *computes* (the systolic array, arithmetic intensity, the padding trap),
the network *topologies* (ICI vs DCN; torus vs Boardfly), and how you *provision*
TPUs on GKE.

Each lesson pairs a browser simulation with the *why*, then a hands-on Cloud TPU
codelab. **L200 (single-slice execution), L300 (megascale), and L400 (chaos &
bare-metal control) are rolling out next.**
